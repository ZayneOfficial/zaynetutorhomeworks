Integers • Exponents • Patterns • Algebraic Expressions

2. Calculate: 18 - 35

5. Simplify: 2³ × 2²
6. Simplify: 5⁶ ÷ 5²
7. Calculate: 3⁰ + 4²

9. Simplify: 4x + 7x - 3
10. Simplify: 8a - 3a + 6
11. Solve: x + 12 = 25

Homework 2

Algebra • Equations • Geometry • Measurement

1. Simplify: 5x + 3x - 7
2. Expand: 4(x + 3)
3. Expand: 2(3x - 5)
4. Solve: 2x + 5 = 17
5. Solve: 5x - 10 = 25
6. Solve: 3x + 4 = 19
7. Factorise: 6x + 12
8. If two angles on a straight line are 65° and x°, find x.
9. The angles of a triangle are 50°, 60° and x°. Find x.
10. Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 14 cm and width 8 cm.
11. Find the area of a rectangle measuring 12 cm × 7 cm.
12. Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 8 cm.
13. Convert 3.5 m into centimetres.
14. A square has a side length of 9 cm. Calculate its perimeter.
15. A rectangle has an area of 72 cm² and a width of 8 cm. Find its length.

Homework 3

Statistics • Probability • Geometry • Data Handling

1. Find the mean of: 5, 8, 10, 12 and 15.
2. Find the median of: 4, 7, 9, 12 and 15.
3. Find the mode of: 3, 5, 5, 7, 8, 5, 9.
4. Find the range of: 12, 18, 25, 9 and 30.
5. A fair coin is tossed once. What is the probability of getting heads?
6. A standard six-sided die is rolled. What is the probability of rolling a 4?
7. A bag contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. What is the probability of selecting a blue ball?
8. What is the sum of the interior angles of a triangle?
9. What is the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?
10. Calculate the circumference of a circle with diameter 14 cm. Use π = 22/7.
11. Calculate the area of a circle with radius 7 cm. Use π = 22/7.
12. A rectangular prism has length 10 cm, width 5 cm and height 4 cm. Calculate its volume.
13. A survey records these numbers of favourite sports: Soccer = 20, Rugby = 10, Cricket = 5. How many learners were surveyed?
14. In a class of 40 learners, 24 passed a test. What percentage passed?
15. A learner scored: 65%, 72%, 80% and 83%. Calculate the mean percentage.
